Honest math · $3,000 ad spend

Worst case first. Then we beat it.

First month leads are usually the more expensive ones. That’s normal. Never judge month one. Look at a 90-day horizon. North star is cost per funded loan — not the cheapest click.

Half of those 25 should be worth a file. One $7k commission already covers the month.

What I will not sell you

This is not cheap and it is not a straight line up. First couple of months you are planting. You might submit nothing in month one. You might submit a stack. Reverse takes hand-holding — 2.5 months to fund is a fair worst-case sales cycle. Price that in whether you work with us or anyone else.

Cost per funded, on a six-month horizon, typically dances around ~$1,100 and can run as high as ~$2,500 in competitive California. More states usually makes that number better, not worse. Starting at $3k spend is a strong start — you said you wanted the best shot, not the cheapest one. We still launch light the first week on purpose so we don’t burn the account while we collect data.
