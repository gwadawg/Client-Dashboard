Pillar 04 · Visibility

Cost per funding — not vanity CPL

You’re analytical. So are we. North star is how much cash in the engine to get a funded deal out — not “leads this month.” We track conversations, shows, submissions, and feed outcomes back into Meta so the account gets sharper.

Targets we’re betting on
DSCR cost per submission ~$1,200 avg. Max cost per funding we want to see: ~$1,600. Even at $2k CPF you’re still profitable vs $7k–$9k DSCR comps.
3X mindset
