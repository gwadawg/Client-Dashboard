Straight Answer First

What you'll earn

We put the number up front because we'd rather you decide early whether this fits. R$5.000–7.000 per month once you're ramped and running accounts independently.

The structure is base plus commission. Commission is tied to your accounts hitting their cost-per-lead targets, so strong months pay more than quiet ones.

The range moves with the agency. More clients means more accounts under your management and more commission. Strong creative work is the other lever — more on that below.

The Job

What you own

Seven areas, and they're genuinely yours. You're the person accountable for Meta performance at this company — nobody is going to sit above you approving every budget change.

01

Meta buying, full book

Every client account. Budgets, campaign structure, targeting, testing, scaling what works, killing what doesn't.

02

CPL & CPQL outcomes

Each account has a written cost-per-lead target. Hitting those targets across the book is how the role is graded.

03

Creative strategy

Read performance data, identify what's actually working and why, then develop new angles and concepts — including writing the scripts.

04

Basic statics

Produce simple static ads using our documented process. AI image prompting is the skill here, not graphic design.

05

Funnel test ideas

Write new headlines and concepts to test in client funnels. You propose and write them; someone else implements.

06

Client ad updates

Proactively tell clients what's working, what changed, and what you're testing. Keeping them in the loop is part of the job.

07

New account launches

The ads side of onboarding a new client — campaign builds and launch. CRM and booking tech is handled by ops.

Just As Important

What you don't own

Roles fail when the edges are vague. These are explicitly not your responsibility, and we won't quietly slide them onto your plate six months in.

  • Video & UGC production You can concept and script it. Someone else produces it.
  • Funnel building You write the test ideas. Implementation happens elsewhere.
  • Client relationships Retention calls and account relationships belong to Client Success.
  • CRM, bots & booking tech Owned by operations. Not your inbox.
  • Non-Meta channels No Google, no TikTok, no LinkedIn. Meta only.
Incentives, Plainly

How you're measured vs. how you grow

Two different things, and we keep them separate on purpose.

The Standard

Measured on performance

CPL and CPQL against written targets. Every account you manage has a number on file. Hitting those numbers consistently is what keeps the seat.

It's the clearest signal we have, it's not subjective, and you'll always know where you stand without waiting for a review.

The Upside

Raised on creative thinking

The quality of your ideas is how you earn more. Angles and concepts you developed from real performance data, that got tested, and that moved the numbers.

Producing statics is table stakes — anyone can follow the process. Knowing what to make and why is the rare part, and it's what we pay up for.

The Senior Bar

What you should already be able to do

This is a senior seat, so we're expecting someone who can run without supervision from early on. Concretely, that means:

  • Run Meta ad accounts independently, without needing direction on daily decisions
  • Diagnose underperformance with evidence — is it the creative, the offer, the audience, or the funnel?
  • Research ads (ad libraries, competitors, our own winners) and turn patterns into concepts worth testing
  • Write ad scripts and concepts clear enough for someone else to produce
  • Prompt AI image tools well enough to ship clean static ads
  • Communicate with clients about their ads clearly, without overpromising
  • Spot a problem and arrive with a proposed solution, not just the problem

Not required on day one

Don't rule yourself out over these — they're trainable, and we'll teach them.

  • Deep graphic design or Canva craft
  • Video editing skills
  • Funnel platform administration
  • Reverse mortgage or DSCR product knowledge

In Short

Who this is really for

This is more than a media buyer seat. We can find people who log into Ads Manager and adjust budgets. What's harder to find is someone who looks at the data and has an opinion about what to do next — who notices a pattern across accounts, connects it to something they saw in the market, and comes back with an idea worth testing.

If you're the kind of person who finds problems and shows up with solutions attached, who gets genuinely interested in why one ad outperformed another, and who wants the room to shape how a growing agency runs its creative — this seat is built for you.

The performance targets keep you honest. The creative work is where you grow.
